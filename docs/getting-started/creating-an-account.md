# Creating an Account

Accounts belong to a server, not a device: you can sign in to the same account on several devices, and each keeps its own session.

## Terms of Use

Before the first sign-in or registration on a device, daccord shows its **Terms of Use & Community Guidelines** (no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour, and how to report and block). Nothing in the signed-out flow, including the server browser, opens until you accept. You only see this again if the terms change; the full text is in **Settings → About** and linked under the sign-in button.

If the server has its own Terms of Service, registering shows a separate checkbox for them.

## Signing In

Choose **Sign In**, enter your username (or email) and password, and submit. You stay signed in until your session expires or you remove the server.

## Registering

1. Choose **Register**.
2. Enter a **username** (your unique identifier on the server).
3. Enter a **password**, or click the dice button to generate a strong one. The eye button reveals it.
4. Optionally enter a **display name**; otherwise your username is shown.
5. Click **Register**.
