# Direct Messages

Direct messages (DMs) are private one-on-one or group conversations outside space channels.

## Opening Direct Messages

Click the **Direct messages** button at the top of the space bar. The panel has two tabs: **Messages** (your conversations, with unread indicators and a search field) and **Friends** (your friends list, where you can also start DMs).

## Starting a Conversation

Right-click (long-press) a member in a member list and choose **Direct Message**, use the button on their profile popout, or start one from the Friends tab.

DMs use the same composer and timeline as channels (Markdown, attachments, reactions, replies, threads, edits, pins, and so on). Start a voice or video call from the conversation header.

## Closing a DM

Right-click (long-press) a conversation and choose **Close direct message**. Starting a DM with that person again brings back its history. For a group DM, choose **Leave group**.

## Group DMs

The group owner can add or remove participants and rename the group. Other participants can view the member list or leave.

## User Menus

Right-click (long-press) a DM recipient, message author, friend, or group member to view their profile, start a DM, manage friendship or blocking, report them, or copy their user ID or username.

## Reporting and Blocking

Report a message from its actions menu. The message is hidden from your view and the dialog offers to block the sender, pre-selected in DMs because there are no moderators to act on it. See [Moderation](../administration/moderation.md#reports).
