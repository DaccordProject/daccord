# Moderation

Each action requires the matching permission for your role.

## Member Actions

Click a member in the member list to open their profile popout:

- **Time out…** -- temporarily stop them sending messages, from 60 seconds to 1 week (**Remove timeout** ends it early).
- **Kick member** -- remove them from the space; they can rejoin with an invite.
- **Ban member** -- remove them and prevent them from rejoining.

## Managing Bans

**Space settings → Banned members** lists banned users with search. Unban one, or select several and click **Unban selected**.

## Deleting Messages

Moderators can delete any message in channels they moderate from the message's actions menu.

## Reports

**Filing a report.** Choose **Report** from a message's actions menu (long-press on touch; right-click or **⋯** on desktop), or **Report user** on a profile popout or a DM user menu. Pick a reason, add optional detail, and submit. Reporting works in spaces and DMs.

A reported message is immediately hidden from your own view on that device, whatever moderators decide. The dialog also offers to **block** the account; this is pre-selected outside spaces, because a DM has no moderators.

**Where a report goes.** Reports filed in a space go to its moderators. Reports filed outside a space go to the server operator, if the server accepts account-level reports; if it doesn't, the dialog says so, and the block and local hide still apply.

**Reviewing reports.** With the `ban_members` permission, open **Space settings → Reports**. Filter by status, then dismiss, resolve, or act on a report (delete the message, kick, or ban), which also closes it.

## Audit Log

**Space settings → Audit log** lists administrative actions with who did what and when. Filter by action type or search by user.
